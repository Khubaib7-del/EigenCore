# Code of conduct

EigenCore follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short, project-sized version of it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in EigenCore to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique code and claims, not people. Disagreement backed by evidence is welcome.
- Accept correction gracefully, including about numbers you published.
- Help newcomers; this is a learning-friendly project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, and personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer (contact listed in `pyproject.toml`). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban participants, depending on severity, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
